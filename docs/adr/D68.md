## D68: Sources of truth: al-Budūr and al-Futūḥ, by al-Shāṭibiyyah and al-Durrah; KFGQPC as a check
Status: accepted (Yusuf and Eisa, 2026-11-23; relayed by Eisa)
Date: 2026-11-23
Context: The app is to show all 20 riwāyāt with every wajh their ṭuruq allow. The KFGQPC muṣḥafs
cover 8 riwāyāt and print one wajh per place. For example, al-Budūr gives Qālūn and al-Dūrī ʿan Abī
ʿAmr three wujūh where هأنتم and هؤلاء meet, each on his own munfaṣil length
([Aljam3, al-Budūr p. 65](https://aljam3.com/ar/4983/12844/65)), and a printed text can show one.
Options considered for the sources of truth: al-Qāḍī's *al-Budūr al-Zāhira* ("min ṭarīqay
al-Shāṭibiyyah wa-al-Durrah", in its title); *al-Futūḥ al-Bāhira* (all 20 ruwāt, extracted in D66);
Ibn Mihrān's *al-Mabsūṭ fī al-Qirāʾāt al-ʿAshr* (d. 381); al-Nashshār's *al-Budūr al-Zāhira*
(d. 938, a different book with the same title); the KFGQPC texts.
Evidence on the excluded books: Ibn Mihrān wrote two centuries before al-Shāṭibī; his editor says
he follows Ibn Mujāhid's method and records many riwāyāt per qāriʾ rather than two ruwāt
([Aljam3, al-Mabsūṭ p. 5](https://aljam3.com/ar/3515/8436/5)). Passages of al-Nashshār's edition
cite works outside the Shāṭibiyyah line (al-Kifāyah, al-Kāmil, al-Mubhij) and al-Nashr
([Aljam3 vol. 2 p. 331](https://aljam3.com/ar/16738/37018/331)); at least some of this may be the
editor's notes. Either way neither book is scoped to al-Shāṭibiyyah and al-Durrah.
Decision:
- Ṭuruq: al-Shāṭibiyyah (the seven) and al-Durrah (the three). Al-Ṭayyibah out of scope for now.
- Sources of truth: al-Qāḍī's al-Budūr, the 1401/1981 Dār al-Kitāb al-ʿArabī print, and al-Futūḥ
  (`book-al-futuh-al-bahira-vol1..6`). The owner's EPUB of al-Budūr (`book-al-budur-al-zahirah`,
  turath.io book 57) is not available (G-225), so the copy used is Aljam3's of the same print
  ([book 3480](https://aljam3.com/ar/3480)): the scanned PDF (`book-al-budur-aljam3-pdf`) and its
  OCR text (`book-al-budur-aljam3-txt`), pinned 2026-11-23 (owner decision). File pages equal the
  printed pages (113 of 113 pages that print a number). The OCR text has errors: a citation is
  checked against the PDF page. The author states the book's ṭuruq in its introduction: al-Taysīr
  and al-Taḥbīr, al-Shāṭibiyyah and al-Durrah ([p. 5](https://aljam3.com/ar/3480/8362/5)).
  The 1,008-page two-volume edition of al-Qāḍī's book on Aljam3
  ([16689](https://aljam3.com/ar/16689)) is the same text and may be used to check OCR and typos,
  not as a second source.
- A fact both books state is `cross-source`; one only one states is `single-source`; a
  contradiction goes to the owner.
- Excluded: Ibn Mihrān's al-Mabsūṭ and al-Nashshār's al-Budūr (other ṭuruq).
- KFGQPC: a check only. With every option at the wajh KFGQPC prints, our output must equal its
  text; a mismatch is our error or a wajh our data lacks, never a reason to override the books.
Consequences: Importers produce candidate rows with page references; Aljam3 text is OCR, so a
citation is checked against the page image. D66's extraction is the al-Futūḥ importer's input.
