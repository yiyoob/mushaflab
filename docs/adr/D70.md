## D70: Owner sign-off is per table, with excepted rows
Status: accepted (Yusuf and Eisa, 2026-11-23; relayed by Eisa)
Date: 2026-11-23
Context: Rule 4 keeps `cross-checked` and `muqri-verified` in `data/approvals/`, set only by the
owner. With tens of thousands of corpus rows, approving row by row would stall every milestone.
Options: approve each row; approve a whole table (a uṣūl category, a surah's farsh, a profile) at
once, pulling out any row the owner flags.
Decision: Per table, with exceptions. An approval names a table file, the sha256 of the content it
approves, the status, and the rows it excepts; excepted rows keep their own status until approved
on their own. A changed table (different sha256) falls back to its rows' own statuses until it
is approved again.
Consequences: `mushaf check` reports which tables are approved, stale or never approved. The
approvals format is in `docs/formats.md`; Claude never edits `data/approvals/` (rule 4).
