## Farsh entries written

25 khilaf places in the range from al-Futuh (`corpus/farsh/00*.yaml`),
23 of them naming Warsh in a variant; an al-Futuh row per variant.
al-Budur pages where the place's Hafs word appears (OCR) are candidates only.
2 printed words could not be split into letters with the codepoint
table and are marked `unreadable` (al-Futuh's glyph decoding, G-219): `2:13:16`, `2:14:16`.

Book rows whose word differs from KFGQPC are expected at this stage: al-Futuh prints its
variants in Madinah dabt, KFGQPC's Warsh mushaf in Maghribi dabt. Closing that gap is the
Warsh writing-style table's job (M2), not the readings'.
